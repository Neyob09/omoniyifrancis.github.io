Upward Bound Mathematics Instruction

University of Alabama at Birmingham

Approach to Academic Service

I am particularly interested in service activities that connect mathematics with students who may not ordinarily see themselves as part of the mathematical community. Whether through competitions, outreach presentations, tutoring, or college-preparation programs, I value opportunities to make mathematics approachable while preserving its intellectual depth.

As I continue my academic career, I hope to contribute to departmental and university service involving undergraduate mathematics education, student success, curriculum development, outreach, and activities that broaden participation in mathematics and STEM.
