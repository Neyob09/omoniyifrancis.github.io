Welcome

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Applied Mathematics at the University of Alabama at Birmingham, with expected graduation in April 2027.

My research lies at the intersection of mathematical biology, dynamical systems, scientific computing, and longitudinal data analysis. I am particularly interested in developing data-informed mathematical models that connect observed health data with mechanistic models of disease progression and population dynamics.

I am also a mathematics educator with experience teaching courses ranging from developmental algebra through Calculus I. My teaching emphasizes conceptual understanding, active problem solving, mathematical communication, and connections between mathematics, computation, and real-world applications.

Current Work

My dissertation, Mathematical Modeling of Cardiovascular Disease–Mortality Dynamics: A Data-Informed Framework with Physical Activity Effect Modification, combines longitudinal survival analysis with mathematical modeling to investigate physical activity, cardiovascular disease, and mortality.
