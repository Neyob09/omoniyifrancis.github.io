Teaching

My teaching focuses on helping students develop conceptual understanding, confidence, and independence in mathematics. I encourage students to move beyond memorizing procedures by asking why a method works, when it should be used, and how mathematical results can be interpreted.

My teaching experience spans developmental algebra, precalculus, trigonometry, and Calculus I. I have worked with students with a wide range of mathematical preparation and academic goals, and I aim to maintain rigorous expectations while providing clear explanations, structured practice, and opportunities for students to ask questions and receive feedback.

Teaching Approach

I organize my teaching around three goals: conceptual understanding, active student involvement, and independent problem solving. I use examples, questions, graphical representations, discussion, and guided practice to help students connect new ideas with concepts they already know.

Assessment also informs my teaching. Homework, quizzes, examinations, and classroom questions help me identify misconceptions and determine when a topic should be revisited or explained from a different perspective.

Instructional Technology

My teaching experience includes Canvas, ALEKS, MyLab Math through Pearson, iClicker, Poll Everywhere, Desmos, and graphing calculators. I use technology for course organization, practice, formative assessment, immediate feedback, and mathematical visualization.

Mentoring & Student Support

Through office hours, tutoring, and individual meetings, I help students identify areas of difficulty and develop strategies for improving their mathematical understanding. I use questions and guided discussion rather than simply providing answers so that students become increasingly independent problem solvers.
