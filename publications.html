Publications & Presentations

My scholarly work spans mathematical biology, disease modeling, optimal control, computational methods, and data-informed approaches to population health. My publications include mathematical models of obesity, diabetes, and hepatitis B, as well as interdisciplinary work involving data science and deep learning.
